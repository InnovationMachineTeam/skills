# Business, Growth, and Communication

## Creating a startup

At idea stage, use a single startup-discovery agent or a small Discovery Team.
Do not build an Agentic OS before persistent cross-functional loops exist.

### Lean initial team

| Role | Outputs |
|---|---|
| Opportunity researcher | market/problem evidence, alternatives, constraints |
| Customer discovery lead | interview plan, evidence, segments, jobs/needs |
| Product strategist | value proposition, assumptions, options, decisions |
| Experiment designer | tests, metrics, stopping rules, results |
| Technical feasibility advisor | architecture options, cost and risk bounds |
| Business model analyst | channels, economics, scenarios, runway assumptions |
| Evidence challenger | contradiction, bias, missing evidence, decision quality |

Required documents include founder intent and constraints, opportunity brief,
assumption map, research protocol/evidence, market/competitor landscape, value
proposition, business model, experiment backlog, metric definitions, risk and
decision logs, product strategy, roadmap options, and investor narrative with
source-backed claims.

```text
Use agent-team-manager to design the minimum startup discovery team for a B2B
agent-evaluation product. Create private domain and interview-synthesis skills,
public evidence skills only when reusable, and stop at a proceed/pivot/stop
decision with experiments. Do not fabricate market size or contact customers.
```

## Discovery Team

A Discovery Team continuously reduces product risk before and during Delivery.

Roles: product/discovery lead, researcher, product analyst, designer/prototyper,
technical feasibility advisor, data/experiment analyst, evaluator, and human
decision owner. Small teams can combine compatible roles, but evidence capture
and decision authority remain distinct.

Artifacts: opportunity backlog, assumptions by value/usability/feasibility/
viability/risk, research evidence, prototype, experiment cards, results,
decision log, updated requirements and delivery candidates.

```text
Use agent-team-manager to design and stage a Discovery Team for the current
product. Inspect the repository and supplied research, define the minimum roles,
skills, docs, model policies, workflows, and handoffs, and propose three cadence
options. Do not create implementation tasks until a human approves the discovery
decision.
```

## Business tracking

One business-tracking agent can maintain an operating review when data sources,
metric definitions, and permissions are bounded. Use a team when finance,
product, marketing, sales, and customer-success data require separate owners or
interpretation.

The contract should include:

- metric dictionary with formula, owner, source, cadence, and limitations;
- targets, baselines, thresholds, and alert policies;
- initiatives, owners, dependencies, risks, and decisions;
- financial runway and scenario assumptions;
- evidence freshness and data-quality status;
- weekly/monthly review package and action log.

```text
Create one business-review agent that reads approved metric exports and plans,
checks freshness and definition drift, and writes a weekly review under
docs/business/reviews/. It can flag anomalies and propose questions but cannot
change targets, spend money, or message external stakeholders.
```

## Marketing team

Suggested roles:

- market/customer insight;
- positioning and messaging;
- content/editorial strategy;
- campaign planner/operator;
- channel specialists as justified;
- creative producer;
- lifecycle/CRM owner;
- marketing analyst and experiment designer;
- brand/legal/privacy reviewer;
- orchestrator and human budget/publish owner.

Create shared public skills for stable brand-safety, evidence, experiment, or
measurement contracts only when multiple agents consume them. Campaign briefs,
client voice, channel procedures, and proprietary examples normally remain
private.

Documents: audience/segment evidence, positioning, messaging house, brand voice,
claims/source register, editorial calendar, campaign briefs, asset variants,
channel plans, experiment cards, consent/privacy checks, metric definitions,
results, learnings, and archive/withdrawal records.

```text
Use agent-team-manager to design a marketing team for a private B2B marketplace
launch. Include research, positioning, content, campaign operations, analytics,
brand/legal review, and independent claims verification. Create necessary skills
with correct visibility. Require human approval for budgets, publishing,
outreach, and use of personal data.
```

## Public-speaking preparation

One speaking-coach agent is usually enough for one speaker and one talk. Use a
team for a high-stakes keynote with research, narrative, visuals, rehearsal,
fact-checking, accessibility, production, and communications owners.

### One-agent package

- audience, venue, format, goal, and constraints;
- talk promise and one-sentence thesis;
- narrative arc and timed outline;
- evidence, examples, stories, and source checks;
- slide/content brief;
- opening, transitions, closing, and call to action;
- anticipated questions and answer boundaries;
- rehearsal plan, timing log, feedback rubric;
- delivery, voice, presence, accessibility, and contingency checklist;
- post-talk reflection and revision notes.

```text
Create one public-speaking coach agent for a 20-minute technical conference
talk. Give it private skills for narrative review, timing, rehearsal feedback,
and Q&A preparation. It may analyze supplied recordings and slides but cannot
publish, submit, or contact organizers. A human speaker approves every claim and
final artifact.
```

### High-stakes team

Roles may include researcher/fact checker, speechwriter, narrative editor,
visual designer, rehearsal coach, subject/security/legal reviewer, event
producer, and red-team interviewer. Keep the speaker as accountable owner.

## Business and communication gates

- external communication, publication, spending, outreach, and PII use require
  explicit authority;
- market and performance claims have evidence and review dates;
- dashboards expose definitions and data quality, not only numbers;
- experiments have hypotheses, primary metrics, guardrails, and stopping rules;
- brand voice does not override truth, consent, legal, or accessibility rules;
- agents recommend decisions; accountable humans own consequential commitments.
