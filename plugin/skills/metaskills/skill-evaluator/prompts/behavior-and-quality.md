# Route: behavior-and-quality

Author or evaluate observable post-activation behavior.

1. Derive cases from real tasks, failures, corrections, traces, runbooks, and intended user outcomes.
2. Use deterministic assertions for files, schemas, tool calls, side effects, and invariants.
3. Use anchored rubrics and blinded pairwise or human review for subjective quality.
4. Add malformed, boundary, adversarial, recovery, and composition cases.
5. Compare with no-skill or previous-version baselines in the same environment.
6. Inspect raw outputs and disagreement before accepting aggregate scores.

Do not encode placeholder quality as a golden answer or let a model judge serve as the only blocking oracle without calibration.
