# Integration and Dispatch Prompt

Convert selected harvest candidates into bounded downstream handoffs without implementing or installing them by assumption.

For every handoff, include candidate IDs, evidence and locators, intended outcome, target host or skill, rights, assumptions, risks, allowed files, preserved invariants, acceptance checks, and forbidden side effects.

Route opportunity discovery to `skill-scout`, creation to `skill-architect`, unsafe or broken material to `skill-doctor`, measured improvement to `skill-optimizer`, topology changes to `skill-refactor`, and lifecycle actions to `skill-manager`. Retain unresolved authority and rights decisions for the user.
