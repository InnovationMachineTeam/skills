# Synthesis and Deduplication Prompt

Cluster candidates by semantic outcome, preconditions, effects, and validation. Preserve every source lineage and distinguish independent recurrence from copied duplication.

Create canonical candidates only when no material constraint is lost. Keep variants for host, risk, authority, reversibility, or environment differences. Surface unresolved contradictions and rejected candidates with reasons.

Prioritize by evidence fidelity, portability, usefulness, safety, rights, testability, and maintenance cost—not raw frequency alone.
