# Harvest Taxonomy

## Harvest units

### Trigger and routing

Descriptions, user phrasings, negative triggers, route-selection rules, and ambiguity questions that determine when or how a skill activates.

### Workflow and decision pattern

Ordered procedures, gates, heuristics, state transitions, escalation conditions, checkpoints, and recovery paths.

### Knowledge and reference

Domain rules, schemas, terminology, constraints, lookup strategies, and stable factual context. Record freshness requirements and authority.

### Prompt and template

Reusable instruction overlays, intake forms, output contracts, checklists, report structures, and artifact templates. Preserve variable slots and assumptions.

### Script and tool pattern

Deterministic utilities, command patterns, adapters, validation scripts, and integration sequences. Record runtime, dependencies, permissions, and side effects.

### Eval and failure evidence

Positive and negative triggers, behavioral cases, fixtures, graders, rubrics, regression examples, traces, anti-patterns, and observed failures.

### Safety and governance

Authority boundaries, approval gates, data handling, prompt-injection defenses, rollback, auditability, ownership, and lifecycle rules.

### Anti-pattern or rejected approach

A tempting pattern that failed, creates unacceptable risk, duplicates a stronger pattern, or cannot be reused under available rights.

## Maturity

- `observed`: appears in at least one source.
- `recurring`: appears in multiple locations; independence may still be unknown.
- `generalized`: source-specific details have been separated from the portable rule.
- `validated`: tested against representative evidence or cases.
- `adoptable`: validated, rights-cleared, documented, and ready for a downstream decision.

Do not advance maturity merely because wording is repeated. Copied text is one lineage unless independent origin is established.
