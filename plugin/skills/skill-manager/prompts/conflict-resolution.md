# Conflict Resolution Management Prompt

Apply after [base.md](base.md).

Classify name collision, divergent duplicate, intentional override, stale copy, overlapping description, namespace ambiguity, or plugin/builtin collision. Determine actual precedence, owners, consumers, and desired identity.

Compare hashes and behavior before recommending rename, namespace, disable, quarantine, consolidate, or pin. Renaming may break invocation; consolidation belongs to `skill-architect`, `skill-refactor`, or `skill-optimizer` according to the boundary change.

Do not delete a predicted loser. Verify host routing on direct, paraphrased, adjacent, ambiguous, and explicit cases after resolution.
