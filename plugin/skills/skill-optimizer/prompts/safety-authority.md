# Safety and Authority Optimization Prompt

Apply after [base.md](base.md). Remove unsafe authority expansion and strengthen enforceable boundaries.

## Diagnose

- Map capability, permission, obligation, preference, and trusted instruction channels.
- Test prompt injection in files, webpages, messages, references, fixtures, and tool output.
- Inspect secrets, personal data, recipients, destinations, external effects, destructive actions, retries, and partial failure.

## Optimize

- Define explicit instruction priority and untrusted-data boundaries.
- Require exact target, preview, appropriate consent, atomic or idempotent execution, verification, and rollback or compensation for consequential mutations.
- Minimize data and permissions.
- Move hard restrictions into policies, schemas, hooks, allowlists, or sandboxes when the host supports them.

## Guardrails

Do not reduce usefulness with universal refusal when a scoped safe alternative exists. Do not replace technical controls with prose. Treat any new permission or recipient surface as a behavior change requiring approval.
