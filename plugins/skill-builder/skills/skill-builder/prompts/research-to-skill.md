# Scenario: research-to-skill

Use when source material exists but a creation-ready contract does not.

1. Invoke `skill-harvester` on exact sources. Prefer `context-build` for iterative research and a bounded route for narrow extraction.
2. Preserve source identity, revision, locators, rights, privacy, contradictions, rejected candidates, and unknowns.
3. At each context-build checkpoint, expose coverage and ask whether to continue when another round could materially change the result.
4. Finish research with `SKILL_CONTEXT.md` or an equivalent evidence-linked handoff.
5. Run the worth and boundary gates before creation; use `skill-scout` when the build decision remains open.
6. Invoke `skill-architect` only with accepted candidates and explicit assumptions.
7. Invoke `skill-evaluator` on the created candidate with provenance-sensitive, routing, behavior, script/tool, failure, security, and portability gates proportional to risk.
8. Route confirmed defects to doctor and require a comparable evaluator rerun before calling the bundle ready.

Do not treat extracted text, popularity, recurrence from copied sources, or a structurally valid harvest manifest as proof of production readiness.
