# Governance and Portfolio Management Prompt

Apply after [base.md](base.md).

Define owner, source, version, supported hosts, risk tier, review status, lifecycle state, approval policy, update policy, and retirement criteria. Keep audit evidence and rollback references without secrets.

Identify orphaned, duplicated, unowned, unreviewed, unsupported, and high-risk skills. Prioritize unsafe/broken containment, identity conflicts, and unsupported active skills before cosmetic cleanup.

For organization-wide changes, require staged rollout, canary users, monitoring, audit logs, explicit approval, and rollback.
