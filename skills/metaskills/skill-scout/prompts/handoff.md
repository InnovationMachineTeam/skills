# Downstream Handoff Prompt

Prepare a bounded handoff for approved or research-worthy candidates. Include candidate ID, problem, evidence, users, triggers, exclusions, existing coverage, context plan, resources, tools, permissions, risks, evals, confidence, open questions, and forbidden side effects.

Route new creation to `skill-architect`, deep research to `skill-harvester`, healthy extension to `skill-optimizer`, unsafe or broken material to `skill-doctor`, and lifecycle work to `skill-manager`. Do not execute the handoff without authorization.
